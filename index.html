Clear before you commit

Questions importers ask first.

Good sourcing starts with fewer assumptions. Here are the basics.

Do I need a finished product specification?

No. A photo, link, rough description, target price, or sample reference is enough to start a useful conversation.

Will I see more than one supplier?

Our goal is to return a shortlist of viable options so you can compare price, MOQ, lead time, and evidence—not just accept one mystery quote.

When does money move?

You approve the supplier and the scope first. Where escrow is used, payment is held according to the agreed order milestones and inspection conditions.

Can you handle shipping and customs?

Yes. We can coordinate export documents, freight options, customs support, and final delivery so the sourcing decision reflects the route to your door.
