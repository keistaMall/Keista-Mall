How an order moves

From your idea to your inventory.

Every order runs through the same six checkpoints, whether it's a single sample or a full container load. You approve each stage before we move to the next.

1
Brief & sourcing
Tell us what you need: a product photo, a spec sheet, or just a description. We shortlist three to five suppliers across our network that can actually make it, not just list it.Turnaround: 2–4 business days
2
Quotes & negotiation
Your dedicated agent handles negotiation with the supplier on your behalf: price, MOQ, and lead time, all worked out in the supplier's own language, with a single comparison sheet handed back to you.You approve the supplier, we don't pick for you
3
Sample & sign-off
A physical sample ships to you before any bulk order is placed. Nothing goes into production until you've held it in your hands.Sample cost credited back on bulk order
4
Production & escrow
Your payment sits in escrow with Keista Mall, not the factory, and is only released once goods pass inspection, so there's no factory to chase if something's wrong.Deposit + balance held on your terms
5
Inspection & consolidation
Our inspectors check the goods against your spec sheet at the factory, then consolidate with any other orders you have running to split the freight cost.Photo & video report included
6
Shipping & customs
We handle export documentation, freight booking, and customs clearance on your end, and hand you a tracking number the moment the container leaves port.Sea, air, or express: your call
What we handle

Everything between finding the right supplier and it sitting in your warehouse.

Use one service or all six. Most clients start with sourcing and add inspection and logistics once the first order lands.

Supplier sourcing & vetting

We visit factories and workshops, check business licences, and confirm production capacity before a supplier ever reaches your shortlist, wherever in the world they're based.

Price & MOQ negotiation

Local presence means fewer "foreigner prices." We negotiate in the supplier's own language and push for MOQs that fit a first-time order.

Quality control & inspection

Pre-production, in-line, and pre-shipment checks against your spec sheet, with a photo and video report for every batch.

Escrow & payment protection

Your funds are released to the factory only after goods pass inspection, so you're never wiring a deposit on trust alone.

Freight, customs & delivery

Sea or air freight, consolidation with other clients' cargo, export paperwork, and customs clearance handled at your destination port.

Private label & packaging

Custom packaging, logo printing, and barcode/labelling setup, arranged directly with the factory during production.

Why work through an agent

The risk isn't finding a supplier. It's what happens after you pay one.

Most import problems trace back to one thing: paying a factory directly, with no one on the ground to hold them accountable.

We're based where your goods are made

Our team is on the ground across Asia, Europe, and the Americas. We can visit a factory floor the same week an issue comes up, not schedule a video call three weeks out.

You never pay a factory directly

All payments route through Keista Mall's escrow. If goods fail inspection, your money doesn't leave escrow. Full stop.

One point of contact, start to finish

The same agent handles your sourcing, sampling, production, and shipping, so your order never gets passed between departments or lost in a handoff.
